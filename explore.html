About us

Who
we are

We take students to the places where transatlantic policy is made, and bring the people who make it to campus.

Sciences Po's Reims campus has hosted the Euro-American programme since 2010. Our members study across the campus's programmes and come from all over Europe and North America.

The bureau 2026–27

Our team

23 students across three poles: events, communications and partnerships.

Co-President

Emma Gianfermi

Paris, France

Second-year, Politics and Government major with an International Relations minor and a North America focus. Emma joined Transatlantic for first-hand insight into diplomacy, especially through conversations with diplomats and ministers at embassy visits and networking events. First as a Partnerships Associate and now as Co-President, she works directly with our international and institutional partners to organise events and grow the network. She cares most about the growing affective polarisation of voters, particularly in the United States, and what it means for diplomacy and international cooperation.

Co-President

Johanna Moora

Tallinn, Estonia

Second-year, MINAN. Johanna joined Transatlantic to get more involved in campus life and make transatlantic issues tangible for students through our events. She has also discovered she loves organising, and feels very “important and professional” sending emails to the team. As an Estonian, she cares deeply about the war in Ukraine and how it shapes the debate on the Russian threat, security and defence. Speaks Estonian, English, French (level still debatable), some Swedish and Russian, and is learning Italian. Fun fact: she nearly chose her third-year exchange university based on whether it had an a cappella group (now it’s folk dancing).

Treasurer

Giacomo Moia

Milan, Italy

Second-year, MINAF, Economy and Society. Giacomo joined Transatlantic to take part in visits to embassies and international institutions, deepen his knowledge of international relations and engage directly with practising diplomats. Speaks Italian, German and English.
